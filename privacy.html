Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Verjus ("the App") is developed by Synapse Ventures AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Authentication provider information (if using Sign in with Apple, a private relay email may be used)

2.2 Content You Create

Verjus stores the following data you voluntarily enter into the app:

  • Recipes (including photos, ingredients, instructions)
  • Meal plans and schedules
  • Grocery lists and items
  • Pantry inventory
  • Wine tasting notes, ratings, and collections
  • Household tasks and assignments
  • Dietary preferences and restrictions
  • Family group information

2.3 Device and Usage Data

We may collect:

  • App version and device type (for compatibility and support)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data (if you opt in)

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Verjus service:

  • Core functionality: Storing and syncing your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, and other content across your devices.
  • Family sharing: Enabling shared access to family content among family group members you invite.
  • AI features: Recipe extraction from photos and aisle classification. On-device processing uses Apple Foundation Models and runs entirely on your device. Cloud-based AI classification sends only the item name (not your account or other personal data) to our classification service.
  • Subscriptions: Managing your subscription tier. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple — we do not collect or store payment information.

4. Data Storage and Sync

Verjus uses a local-first architecture:

  • Your data is stored primarily on your device using Apple SwiftData.
  • If you are part of a family group, your data is synced to our backend service (hosted on Supabase) to enable sharing with family members.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Data at rest in Supabase is encrypted.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Verjus:

  • Supabase: Backend database and authentication. Your account data and shared family content are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Apple: Payment processing for subscriptions via StoreKit, and Sign in with Apple for authentication. Apple Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Data stored locally on your device remains under your control and can be deleted by removing the app.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data associated with your account (available directly in the app).
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data at any time via the Settings screen in the app.
  • Export: Request a copy of your data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Correct: Update or correct your data through the app at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Verjus is designed for family use, but account creation requires an adult (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use of Verjus after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:
