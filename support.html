Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a family group?

Open the app, go to Settings > Family, and tap "Create Family." You'll receive a 6-digit join code to share with family members. They can join by entering that code in their app.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers. Data stored locally on your device will be removed when you delete the app.

Does Verjus work offline?

Yes. Verjus stores your data locally on your device first. If you're part of a family group, changes will sync automatically when you're back online.

How do subscriptions work?

Verjus offers three subscription tiers: Plus, Pro, and Premium. Each unlocks additional features like advanced AI recipe extraction, additional family members, and enhanced wine tracking. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and can be canceled anytime in your device Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Install Verjus on your new device, sign in with the same Apple ID, then go to Settings > Subscription > Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be restored automatically.

Is my data shared with other family members?

Only if you create or join a family group. When you add content to a shared family, it becomes visible to all members of that family. Personal data outside the family group remains private.

What AI features does Verjus use?

Verjus uses on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) for recipe extraction from photos and aisle classification of grocery items. Processing happens entirely on your device — no data is sent to external AI services for these features.
